Based on Article 13 of the Swiss Federal Constitution and the data protection provisions of the Swiss Confederation (Data Protection Act, DSG), every person is entitled to protection of their privacy and protection against misuse of their personal data. We comply with these provisions: personal data is treated in strict confidence and is neither sold nor disclosed to unrelated third parties without a legal basis.


## 2. Local Data Storage

The app stores your data **locally on your device** by default:

- **Wallet data** (private keys, recovery words) is stored in an encrypted local database.
- **PIN** is stored exclusively as a cryptographic hash, not in plain text.
- **Settings** (language, currency, network) are stored locally.

RealUnit has **no access** to your private keys, your recovery words (seed phrase), or your PIN.


## 3. Collection and Sharing of Personal Data

Registration is required for the purchase and sale of RealUnit share tokens and for entry in the share register. The following personal data is collected:

- First and last name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Residential address
- Date of birth
- Citizenship
- Account type (individual or corporation)
- Tax residence (for countries other than Switzerland, with tax number)
- Wallet address

This data is transmitted to and processed by the following companies:

- **RealUnit Schweiz AG** – for maintaining the share register and fulfilling legal obligations (e.g. reporting obligations, AEOI)
- **DFX AG** – on our behalf, for processing buy and sell transactions as well as for conducting identity verification (KYC) and for screening against sanctions/embargo lists (sanctions screening)
- **DFX Services AG** – on our behalf, for matching incoming payments on our account with Hypothekarbank Lenzburg AG (name and account of the ordering party, amount, reference)
- **Aktionariat AG** – as registrar, which maintains the share register and the register of ledger-based securities on our behalf, and for technical support of the tokenization. If, in addition to the RealUnit App, you use your own account with Aktionariat AG (such as the Aktionariat app or the investor page), Aktionariat AG processes the data of this account under its own responsibility in accordance with its own privacy policy (https://www.aktionariat.com/privacy-policy). This includes that changes you make in your account are also updated in the share registers of other companies in which you are registered via Aktionariat AG.
- **Hypothekarbank Lenzburg AG** – as the bank holding our account for deposits (payment transactions)

Where provided for by law, we also disclose this data to authorities and bodies, in particular to the Swiss Federal Tax Administration (FTA) as part of the automatic exchange of information, to the State Secretariat for Economic Affairs (SECO) in the event of sanctions matches, to the Money Laundering Reporting Office Switzerland (MROS), to law enforcement authorities and courts, and to our self-regulatory organization under the Anti-Money Laundering Act and the auditors appointed by it.

Processing of this data is necessary for initiating and performing the contractual relationship and for fulfilling legal obligations (e.g. reporting and retention obligations); insofar as we process data based on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time. For users within the scope of the GDPR, processing is based on Art. 6 para. 1 lit. a, b and f GDPR; our legitimate interest lies in fulfilling the Swiss legal obligations applicable to us, in particular with regard to maintaining the share register, combating money laundering, complying with sanctions and tax reporting.


## 4. Identity Verification (KYC)

Identity verification (Know Your Customer, KYC) is required for purchases and sales regardless of the amount. This is carried out through DFX AG, with Sumsub being used as the technical service provider for identity verification. Additional data may be collected, in particular:

- Identity documents (passport, identity card)
- Biometric data for facial recognition (as part of video identification)
- Approximate and precise location (as a fraud-prevention signal, only when the identity verification is started)

DFX AG processes this data on our behalf. If you enter into a separate customer relationship with DFX AG for the exchange of ZCHF into CHF or EUR and the payout to your bank account (off-ramp), DFX AG also processes this data and your registration data (section 3) for this customer relationship under its own responsibility in accordance with its own privacy policy.

The biometric data for facial recognition collected during video identification constitutes sensitive personal data (Art. 5 lit. c no. 4 DSG) or special categories of personal data (Art. 9 GDPR). It is processed with your explicit consent (Art. 9 para. 2 lit. a GDPR).


## 5. Communication with External Services and Disclosure Abroad

- **DFX AG**: For exchange rate queries, balance queries, buy/sell and registration functions, and for crash and error reports. Your wallet address and, where applicable, personal data are transmitted.
- **Ethereum blockchain**: The app queries balances and transaction data via DFX AG and submits signed transactions to the Ethereum network via DFX AG. Only DFX AG receives your IP address in the process.

DFX AG acts on our behalf; regarding its own responsibility for the off-ramp, see section 4.

DFX AG, DFX Services AG, Aktionariat AG and Hypothekarbank Lenzburg AG are domiciled in Switzerland. Some of the aforementioned recipients or their sub-processors may process personal data outside Switzerland, namely in the following countries:

- **Sumsub** (identity verification on behalf of DFX AG): storage and processing in Germany; for individual services, Sumsub may transfer data to the United Kingdom and the USA.
- **dilisense GmbH** (screening against sanctions lists on behalf of DFX AG): processing in Germany.
- **Aktionariat AG**: processing in Switzerland and in member states of the European Union (EU) and the European Economic Area (EEA).
- **Ethereum network**: transactions are distributed via the globally distributed Ethereum network and are public (section 6); they contain your wallet address, but not your name or your IP address.
- **App store operators**: Apple and Google, domiciled in Ireland and the USA.

According to the decision of the Swiss Federal Council, Germany, the other EU and EEA states and the United Kingdom ensure an adequate level of data protection (Art. 16 para. 1 DSG); for the USA, this applies only to recipients certified under the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Where data is disclosed to a country without an adequate level of data protection, appropriate safeguards are ensured, in particular standard contractual clauses (Art. 16 para. 2 lit. d DSG), or the disclosure is based on a statutory exception (Art. 17 DSG); within the scope of the GDPR, Art. 44 et seq. GDPR apply. Tax data is transmitted via the FTA to the partner states of the automatic exchange of information on the basis of international treaties (Art. 16 para. 2 lit. a DSG).


## 6. Blockchain Transparency

All transactions on the Ethereum blockchain are **publicly visible**. This means that your wallet address and all associated transactions can be viewed by anyone. RealUnit has no influence over the public nature of the blockchain.


## 7. Device Permissions

- **Camera**: For scanning QR codes (e.g. for wallet addresses or payment requests)
- **Biometric sensors**: For Face ID or fingerprint unlock (optional)
- **Microphone and location**: For identity verification (section 4)
- **Photos**: For supporting documents in the event of a change of name or address and for attachments to support requests
- **Bluetooth**: For connecting to a BitBox (hardware wallet)

These permissions are optional and can be managed at any time in your device settings; without a permission, the respective function may not be available. The data of the biometric sensors (Face ID, fingerprint) is processed exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to us or third parties.


## 8. Data Security

Communication between the app and external services is carried out via encrypted connections (TLS/SSL). We strive to protect your data as well as possible against unauthorized access, loss or misuse through appropriate technical and organizational measures.


## 9. Your Rights

You have the right at any time to:

- **Request information** about the personal data we have stored
- **Request correction** of inaccurate data
- **Request deletion** of your data, insofar as no statutory retention obligations apply
- **Revoke your consent** to data processing

Insofar as the GDPR applies, you are also entitled to the right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR), the right to object (Art. 21 GDPR) and the right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR). You furthermore have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Switzerland: the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner, FDPIC; within the scope of the GDPR: the competent data protection supervisory authority).

To exercise these rights, please contact info@realunit.ch.
